## Full Description (4000 chars max)
RupeeFlow is a 100% offline, privacy-first personal finance and debt ledger application tailored for Indian financial workflows (paise-precise arithmetic, UPI, net banking, cash, and group splits).

### Key Features

• **Zero-Drift Financial Ledger**
RupeeFlow records every transaction in 64-bit integer paise (₹1 = 100 paise), eliminating floating-point rounding errors. Track personal expenses, group splits, and friend-to-friend debts with mathematical integrity.

• **Friend Groups & Flexible Bill Splits**
Split bills with friends and custom groups (Roommates, Trip to Goa, Office Lunch). Supports:
- Equal splits (with automatic 1-paise discrepancy resolution)
- Exact amount splits
- Percentage splits

• **Smart SMS & Notification Expense Detection (Optional)**
Optionally auto-detect bank debit SMS and notification alerts from Indian banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, PNB, etc.) and UPI apps.
- RupeeFlow extracts only the amount, merchant, and reference number.
- No personal SMS is ever uploaded to servers or shared. Everything processes locally on your device.
- Full prominent disclosure and opt-in consent provided.

• **End-to-End Encrypted Backups**
Export encrypted database backups secured by PBKDF2 with HMAC-SHA256 and authenticated AES-256-GCM. Transfer your financial data securely across devices without relying on third-party cloud servers.

• **100% Offline & Private**
No accounts, no cloud servers, no trackers, no advertisements. Your data stays entirely in local SQLite storage on your device.
